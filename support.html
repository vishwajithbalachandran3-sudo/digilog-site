Returns

Contact us within 30 days of delivery before returning an item. Products should be unused, complete, and in their original packaging. Custom items, consumables, and components damaged by incorrect installation may not be eligible.

  1. Email your order number and reason for return to digilogmicro@info.com.
  2. Wait for return instructions and the return reference.
  3. Package the item securely and include the reference.

Warranty

Products are covered by any warranty stated on the product page or supplied by the manufacturer. Warranty coverage applies to verified defects under normal, specified use.

Coverage does not generally include accidental damage, incorrect wiring, overvoltage, overheating, modification, unsuitable storage, misuse, or normal wear. Keep your order confirmation and provide clear photographs or test results when making a claim.
