Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Digilog Micro Solutions collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, submit a project inquiry, create a shopping cart, or place an order.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Contact information such as your name, email address, company, billing address, and delivery address.
  • Order information, including products, quantities, delivery selections, and order history.
  • Project inquiry information that you choose to include in an email to us.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, referring page, and website activity when analytics are enabled.

Payment card details should be handled by an approved payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Digilog on this website.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested engineering services.
  • To process, deliver, and support product orders.
  • To provide customer service and important order updates.
  • To maintain website security and improve our services.
  • To meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

4. Cookies and local storage

The store may use browser local storage to remember items in your cart. This information remains on your device until it is removed, the order is completed, or browser storage is cleared. We will update this policy if additional analytics or advertising cookies are introduced.

5. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only with service providers needed to operate the business, such as payment processors, delivery companies, hosting providers, and professional advisers, or when required by law.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, contractual requirements, fraud prevention, and applicable law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website and services change. The updated date at the top indicates the latest revision.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email digilogmicro@info.com.
