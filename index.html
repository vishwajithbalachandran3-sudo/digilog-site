ABOUT DIGILOG

Serving power and solar markets since 2012.

Digilog Micro Solutions Pvt. Ltd. was established in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, in 2012. We manufacture, export, and supply power conditioning units, solar monitoring systems, vehicle trackers, charge controllers, and solar street lights.

Our products combine current technology with durability, multiple functions, energy efficiency, and low power consumption. An organized infrastructure spanning production, administration, sales, marketing, packaging, laboratories, and quality control supports our commitment to dependable products and timely supply.
